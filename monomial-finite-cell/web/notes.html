Monomial Game Conventions

The bridge-free rules used by this finite-cell search.

Game

One Monomial Differential

Every attached cell has one A-infinity monomial as its differential. A difference of monomials is a bridge, so bridge cells are unavailable in this game. Ordinary products of generators remain available as seeds; without them no first Massey products could be defined.

One Compatible Massey System

Every operation of arity at least three is represented by its canonical Massey product. All such products are taken in a single compatible defining system, so the program stores their input data and certificates rather than explicit cochain formulas.

Preserved Relations

Stasheff A-infinity replacement relations remain active. Lemma-1 is also active: a completed compatible pair of Massey towers certifies its admissible collapsed monomial product blocks.

Same Completed Tower Counts Once

If two cyclic presentations are eliminated by the same completed tower, the program counts them as one resolution of the cyclic class.
